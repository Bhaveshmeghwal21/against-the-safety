## Hypothesis

Round 3 improved reading but encouraged the shortcut that supplied passages imply answerability. A matched curriculum that makes answerability depend on actual visible facts, balances terminal answers and abstentions at the same search depth, and replays the old search decisions may improve that distinction. A lower learning rate reduces the size of the change. This is a hypothesis, not a result.

## Fixed experiment

- Two initializations: candE from main, candF from round-2 candA. Same training examples, seed 47, learning rate 3e-5, two epochs, rank 16, maximum input/completion length 2560, gradient accumulation 16. Save epoch 1 and epoch 2; at most four checkpoints are selectable.
- About 1.2 million processed tokens per epoch, counted with the real saved tokenizer. Half of the token budget is targeted data, half is replay selected from main's original training rows. Decisions are selected as complete positive/negative pairs at the same search depth: answer versus search at depths 0--2, answer versus abstain at depth 3. Sampling weights over depths 0,1,2,3 are 0.15,0.15,0.10,0.60; terminal answer/abstain pairs receive the largest share. Never select just the easier side of a pair.
- New SQuAD **training-split** articles, excluding the 105 articles assigned to round-3 training/development. Seeded article groups: 60 training, 15 development, 20 final holdout. No natural-unanswerable SQuAD training labels. All variants and compound source questions remain in their article group. Existing evaluation datasets become informed regression checks.
- Targeted positive/negative examples use the same question, passage count, ordering rule, tool schema and search depth. Missing-evidence examples replace a support passage with a real on-topic negative, mask alternate support copies and reject alternate visible answers. Complete examples cover stopping before search and after one, two or three searches; missing-evidence examples teach search before the budget is exhausted and abstention at the end. Any temporary retrieval masks are recorded as training interventions, never printed to the actor.
- Reject overlength paired terminal rows together using actual tokenizer counts. Reject rows with hidden answers in visible evidence or missing answers outside the display limit. Preserve real search results and neutral chunk IDs.
- Before finalizing data, audit another shortcut: at the paired tool-result turn, both labels must introduce the same number of previously unseen chunk IDs. If the negative result would contain only already-shown passages, apply an explicit temporary mask to previously seen negative-only results, shared by both environments, until novelty matches; otherwise reject the pair. The actual unmasked positive ranking is preserved. This additional training intervention prevents the simple rule "new passage appeared, therefore answer". It was introduced before model training, based solely on inspecting the generator.
